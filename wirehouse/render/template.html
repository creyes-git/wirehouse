    WASD
    Walk (Shift to run, Space to jump)
    Drag
    Look around, scroll to zoom
    Click
    Inspect a block, rack or pipe. Click it again to walk there
    E
    Walk into the block in front of you
    Q
    Step back out to the room above
    /
    Find anything and walk straight to it
    P
    Switch between walking and the floor plan
    T
    Switch between night and day shift
    R
    Route from the inspected block: pick an end and walk it
    C
    Clipboard: guided tours and quests
    X
    Export a picture or a share card of what you are looking at
    V
    Changes since an earlier map, when the page has them
    Space , .
    During a tour or route: play or pause, back, next
    M
    Sound effects on or off (off until you turn them on)
    Esc
    Back out one step (stop, close, clear)
