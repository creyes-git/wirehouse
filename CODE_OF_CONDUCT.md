# Code of Conduct

Wirehouse adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is at that link; this page summarises it and says how to
report a problem.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to programming.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences, and giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent
the project).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@__OWNER__](https://github.com/__OWNER__),
through a direct message on GitHub, or by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/__OWNER__/wirehouse/security/advisories/new) if you prefer
not to be identified in public. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit
or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban participants, following the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
